How it's computed. For the chosen speed, the page solves for the release angle that makes a gravity-only path (g = 32.17 ft/s²) pass through your target height at the front of the plate, then reports the angle the ball is falling at when it gets there.

Gravity only. Air drag and spin are left out. A real four-seam fastball's backspin holds it up, so MLB fastballs arrive about 1° to 2° flatter than this model predicts for the same speed and heights (typically 4° to 5° down). Curveballs, with topspin, come in steeper than the model. Slow the pitch down to see gravity alone push the angle past 10°.
